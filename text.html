ABOUT THE ARTIST

With over 15 years of experience in the industry, I specialize in creating custom, one-of-a-kind tattoos that reflect each client's unique personality and story.

My journey began in Tokyo where I apprenticed under master tattoo artists, learning traditional techniques that inform my contemporary practice today.

I believe in a collaborative approach, working closely with clients to transform their ideas into wearable art that stands the test of time.
